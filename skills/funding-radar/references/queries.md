# Funding radar: query patterns and sources

## Query patterns (Exa)

Phrase queries the way the news is written:

- `<geography> <sector> startup raises <stage> round`
- `<sector> startup secures <stage> funding led by`
- `<stage> funding round <sector> <geography> <month year>`

Stages to cover: pre-seed, seed, pre-Series A, Series A, Series B, Series C.

## Where Indian rounds are usually announced

Good for finding rounds, then confirm details on a second source:
Entrackr, Inc42, YourStory, VCCircle, The Economic Times (ET Tech), Moneycontrol, Mint, Business Standard,
TechCrunch (for larger rounds), company and investor blogs, and LinkedIn posts by the founders or the lead fund.

## Confirming a round

- A company or lead-investor post confirms the round.
- Regulatory filings (share allotments in company registry filings) confirm it later, often weeks after the news.
- Tracxn, Crunchbase and similar databases are useful cross-checks but can lag or mislabel stages.
