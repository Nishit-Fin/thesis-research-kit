# Due-diligence checklist

For each check: what to look for, where, and what counts as a red flag.

## 1. Company registry
- **Look for:** legal name and CIN (company number), incorporation date, registered office, status (active, strike-off, under liquidation), directors and their other companies, authorised and paid-up capital, registered charges (secured loans), date of the last annual filing.
- **Where:** the government company registry first; aggregators (Tofler, Zauba Corp, The Company Check) as leads.
- **Red flags:** status not active; annual filings more than a year late; a director disqualified or linked to struck-off companies; registered office at a mass-registration address; charges that suggest heavy secured debt.

## 2. Founders and team
- **Look for:** degrees, past employers and startups, publications, other directorships, who owns the core technology.
- **Where:** LinkedIn, university pages, the registry's director search, past press.
- **Red flags:** claimed degrees or roles that do not match public records; a technical claim with no technical founder; many parallel directorships.

## 3. Patents and trademarks
- **Look for:** each claimed patent's number, status (filed, published, granted, lapsed), owner, filing date, and whether it covers the core product. Trademark for the brand.
- **Where:** IP India public search, Google Patents, WIPO Patentscope, the university's technology-transfer page if the IP came from a lab.
- **Red flags:** "patents" that are only filed; owner is a founder or a university with no licence to the company; patents lapsed for unpaid fees; claimed count differs across sources.

## 4. Research papers
- **Look for:** the team's papers on the core technology, citation counts, and independent work that supports or contradicts the claimed performance.
- **Where:** Firecrawl paper search, arXiv, Semantic Scholar, Google Scholar, journal pages.
- **Red flags:** performance claims far above the best published results with no paper; the key paper is by someone not on the team.

## 5. Tenders and government orders
- **Look for:** bids placed and orders won, buyer, value, date, and whether it was a pilot or a scaled order; grants and programme selections.
- **Where:** GeM, CPPP, SECI, plus sector portals (see `sources-india.md`); grant and programme lists (DST, BIRAC, TDB, iDEX, National Quantum Mission, Startup India).
- **Red flags:** "government customers" with no order on any portal; only bids, never awards; a tender reopened because no domestic supplier qualified.

## 6. Funding history
- **Look for:** each round's date, amount, stage and investors, against the registry's share-allotment filings.
- **Where:** announcements, investor portfolio pages, registry filings, Tracxn or Crunchbase as a cross-check.
- **Red flags:** announced rounds with no matching allotment after several months; round sizes that differ across sources; investors that do not list the company.

## 7. Financials
- **Look for:** revenue, profit or loss, cash, and growth over the last two or three filed years.
- **Where:** annual filings summarised by aggregators; confirm key numbers on the registry where possible.
- **Red flags:** revenue claims far above filed revenue; auditor changes or qualified audit opinions.

## 8. Customers and traction
- **Look for:** named customers, pilots, case studies, repeat orders, active hiring, GST registration status.
- **Where:** company site, customer press releases, job boards, the GST public search.
- **Red flags:** logos with no public confirmation; pilots that never convert; GST registration cancelled.

## 9. Legal and litigation
- **Look for:** cases where the company or founders are a party; insolvency petitions; consumer complaints.
- **Where:** eCourts, Indian Kanoon, NCLT cause lists, consumer forum records.
- **Red flags:** insolvency filings; disputes with former founders over IP; repeated recovery suits by suppliers.

## 10. Regulatory and licences
- **Look for:** the approvals the product legally needs and whether they are held (for example BIS certification, CDSCO for medical devices, RBI licences for payments or lending, DGCA for drones, SCOMET for controlled exports, telecom certification, CERT-In empanelment).
- **Red flags:** selling without a required licence; a licence application pending for a long time with no plan.

## 11. Competition
- **Look for:** Indian and global companies solving the same problem, their funding, and how this company differs.
- **Red flags:** a better-funded competitor with the same approach and a head start; "no competitors".

## 12. Adverse media and sanctions
- **Look for:** fraud allegations, regulator action, customer disputes; sanctioned investors, partners or suppliers.
- **Where:** news search for the company and each founder with words like fraud, dispute, FIR, SEBI, RBI; public sanctions lists for foreign parties.
- **Red flags:** anything unresolved and specific. Rumour without a source is not a finding.
