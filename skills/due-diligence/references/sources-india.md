# Indian public sources, portal by portal

Many government portals use captchas or logins. When Firecrawl cannot read a
page, record "confirm on portal" and give the user the page to open.

| Check | Primary source | Useful leads |
|---|---|---|
| Company registry | Ministry of Corporate Affairs (mca.gov.in), master data and filings | Tofler, Zauba Corp, The Company Check |
| Tax registration | GST public search (gst.gov.in) | none |
| Patents and trademarks | IP India public search (ipindia.gov.in) | Google Patents, WIPO Patentscope |
| Research papers | Journal and arXiv pages | Semantic Scholar, Google Scholar, Firecrawl paper search |
| Government purchases | GeM, Government e-Marketplace (gem.gov.in), bids and orders | news on large orders |
| Central tenders | CPPP, Central Public Procurement Portal (eprocure.gov.in) | tender aggregators as leads only |
| Renewable energy and storage tenders | SECI (seci.co.in), plus NTPC, NHPC and state agencies | industry news (Mercom, ET EnergyWorld) |
| Defence | iDEX winners, DRDO technology-transfer lists, Ministry of Defence tenders | defence press |
| Space | IN-SPACe authorisations, ISRO and NSIL announcements | space press |
| Railways | IREPS (ireps.gov.in) | none |
| Grants and programmes | DST, BIRAC, TDB, National Quantum Mission, Startup India, MeitY programme lists | press releases (pib.gov.in) |
| Courts | eCourts (ecourts.gov.in), Indian Kanoon | news |
| Insolvency | NCLT cause lists, IBBI public announcements | news |
| Securities and lending | SEBI and RBI orders and lists of licensed entities | news |
